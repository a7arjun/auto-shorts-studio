Battle Logic Lab Publisher creates English Pokémon game battle explainers and manages their publication to its operator’s own YouTube channel.

What the application does

The software compares stated legal builds using simulated battles, produces original narration and visual layouts, and prepares short-form videos. Results depend on the selected rules, builds and strategy; they are not canonical anime outcomes.

Why it connects to Google

With the channel owner’s permission, the application uses the YouTube Data API to confirm channel ownership, upload generated videos privately, set titles and descriptions, schedule publication and verify video processing and visibility. Public video metadata supports research into recent, relevant matchup topics.

The requested youtube.force-ssl permission enables the upload and video-management operations used by this workflow. The app does not request access to Gmail, Drive or Google account passwords.

Who can use it

This is a personal-use application operated by the project maintainer for his own channel. This website explains the application; it is not an account signup service and does not collect Google credentials.

Data and control

Authorization credentials are supplied through restricted secret storage. The operator can revoke Google access at any time. Read the Privacy Policy for data handling, service providers and removal requests, and the Terms of Use for the application’s scope.
